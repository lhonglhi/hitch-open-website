Hitch Open Ping-Pong Embodied AI Challenge

Table tennis is the ultimate test of a humanoid robot's speed, adaptability and sustained real-time decision-making. HOPE examines dynamic balance, high-speed motion control, real-time autonomous decisions and scene generalization in a single arena — embodied AI's key leap from merely moving to competing and winning, backed by a quantifiable, reproducible open validation system.
